API Technical Transparency

A precise breakdown of every network call this script makes, for anyone who wants to verify the plain-language disclosure against the actual behavior.

Key Scope
user: battlestats, log
faction: rankedwars, basic

Nothing outside this is ever requested — no messages, money, bazaar, or other players' data, and no write/POST endpoints of any kind. Every call is a plain read-only GET. After generating your key, you can further restrict its log access to Torn's "Gym" category in your own API settings.

Regular Sync
» Every 30 minutes — a background heartbeat while the panel is open. Requests battlestats plus your training/item logs (and, rarely, a 4th call for stat-enhancer logs, only if battlestats shows a stat increase since last sync).
» On Train click — one lightweight call for just that stat's log, debounced 1 second against rapid clicks. Does not request battlestats.
» On page load — if you trained and navigated away before a sync could complete, the next load catches up immediately.
» Manual RESYNC button — same as the 30-minute sync, on demand.

Each open tab keeps its own independent 30-minute timer — with several tabs open, you may see more than one sync over time rather than a single shared one.

Log Types Read
» Training (4 log types) — strength, defense, speed, dexterity.
» Energy items (6 log types) — green eggs, Xanax, LSD, energy cans, hotel coupons, points refills.
» Stat enhancers (4 log types) — parachutes, skateboards, boxing gloves, dumbbells.
» Happy items (4 log types) — candy, erotic DVDs, ecstasy, yellow eggs.
» Overdoses (3 log types) — Xanax, LSD, and Ecstasy ODs.
» Faction history — join/leave record, read once per Backfill run to line up war dates.
BB Backfill
» Walks each log category backward in pages, paced at a fixed 700ms between calls (≈85 calls/minute).
» Budgeted to 40,000 rows/day (stops starting new days past this), with a 42,000-row hard failsafe — kept under Torn's own 50,000-row/day cloud-read cap, which is shared across every script you run.
» If the daily budget is hit, a ~24-hour cooldown is armed before scanning can resume — timed so every counted row has aged out of Torn's rolling 24-hour window.
» A separate, much shorter 45-second lock exists purely to stop two tabs from scanning at once; if a scan is interrupted by a crash or refresh, this lock clears on its own within 45 seconds so Resume isn't blocked. A manual pause has no cooldown at all — Resume is available immediately.
» A scan stops when it reaches the true start of your account history, hits the daily budget, or Torn itself returns a rate-limit/daily-cap error — in every case it stops cleanly rather than retrying blindly.
Staying Within Torn's Limits

Torn enforces roughly 100 API calls/minute and a 50,000-row/day cloud-data read cap per category. This script doesn't track a live request counter against those numbers — instead it stays comfortably under them by design: the 700ms Backfill pacing caps it near 85 calls/minute, the 40,000/42,000-row budget leaves headroom under the daily cap, and regular sync only runs once per 30 minutes plus whatever your own Train clicks add. If Torn ever responds with a rate-limit or daily-cap error anyway, the script stops rather than retrying.
