Purpose: Personal stat tracking, for you only.
Key storage: Stored locally, used only to make automated read-only requests.
Key access level: Custom, a fixed list of specific log types plus battlestats.
What This Script Reads

This script only reads a fixed list of specific logs related to your training data:

Training logs. Your gym workouts.
Energy items. Eggs, Xanax, LSD, cans, FHCs, refills.
Stat enhancers. Parachutes, skateboards, gloves, dumbbells.
Happy items. Candy, DVDs, ecstasy, yellow eggs.
Overdoses. Xanax, LSD, and Ecstasy ODs.
Faction history. One-time read of your join/leave record. Used to line up war dates.
Current stats. Your battlestat totals, to measure gains between syncs.
Faction wars. Your faction's war history, for the calendar.

Your profile, messages, money, and other players' data are outside this scope entirely.

You can verify this yourself by reviewing the source. Search for "THE CHECK-IN COUNTER" to see exactly how your data is handled.
